# AI Acceptable-Use Policy Generator

**Create a clear, plain-English AI use policy for your small business in about 10 minutes. Free, private, no sign-up.**

👉 **[Create your policy](https://alealonso-ai.github.io/ai-policy-generator/)**

---

## Why this exists

Employees at most small businesses are already using AI tools, often personal ChatGPT accounts, with no guidance. That puts customer data, confidential information and the company's reputation at risk.

Large companies have legal teams to write AI policies. Small businesses usually have nothing. This tool closes that gap with a practical starting policy, written in language employees will actually read.

## How it works

1. Answer 11 short questions: your company details, industry, overall approach to AI, approved tools, protected information, allowed and prohibited uses, human review, customer disclosure and training.
2. The policy writes itself live as you answer.
3. **Download it for Word** to edit, **print or save it as a PDF**, or **copy the text** into your handbook.

The finished policy covers purpose and scope, approved tools and accounts, protecting information, approved and prohibited uses, accuracy and human review, transparency with customers, industry-specific rules, training, reporting problems, policy review dates, and an employee acknowledgment.

### Industry-specific rules

Extra rules are added automatically for healthcare, financial services, legal, education, retail and hospitality, professional services, and real estate.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is collected, stored or sent anywhere.

## Important

This tool creates a starting template, **not legal advice**. Have the final policy reviewed by a qualified professional, especially in regulated industries.

## Tech

A single `index.html` file (HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript) with no frameworks or dependencies, hosted free on GitHub Pages.

To edit the industry rules, change the `INDUSTRY_RULES` list in the **CONTENT** section of `index.html`.
